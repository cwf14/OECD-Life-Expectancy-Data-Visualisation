About


Life expectancy, a critical indicator of a population's health and well-being, is significantly influenced by a myriad of factors, including access to quality healthcare and favorable social conditions. Variations in life expectancy across different regions and populations can be attributed to disparities in causes of mortality, health coverage rates, levels of domestic general government health expenditure per capita, and purchasing power parity (PPP).

Global Age Group Indicators Across Countries

This map visualizes age group-related metrics (such as life expectancy or mortality rates) for each country. Each country is color-coded according to the observed value for a selected age group and year, providing a geographical perspective on age-specific trends.

This stacked bar chart illustrates mortality rates per 100,000 inhabitants, categorized by specific causes of death. Each section of the bar represents a distinct cause, such as cardiovascular diseases, respiratory issues, or external injuries.

Government Purchasing Power Parity (PPP) Comparison Across OECD Countries

This spider chart visualizes the government PPP values of selected countries, allowing for a direct comparison of purchasing power across different economic environments. Each axis represents a country's PPP, showing its economic strength in terms of government purchasing power for the latest available year.
